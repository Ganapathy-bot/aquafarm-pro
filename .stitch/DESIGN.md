# Design System: AquaFarm Pro
**Project ID:** 16064670208389050663

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Premium agricultural technology meets a modern aquaculture dashboard. Light, calm, and practical. Production-ready Android mobile UI, not a website or a slide. Realistic Indian freshwater carp farming photography. Readable technical content over decoration.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Deep Ocean** (#0B3D5C) – titles, bottom navigation, primary text on light surfaces
- **Aqua Teal** (#128C7E) – primary buttons, active nav, key highlights
- **Fresh Green** (#3C8D4E) – success, growth, secondary accents
- **Mist** (#E7F3F8) – page background
- **Card White** (#FFFFFF) – cards and sheets
- **Ink** (#143044) – body text
- **Muted** (#5E7382) – supporting labels
- **Caution** (#B45309) – warning cards only

## 3. Typography Rules
Modern sans-serif (Manrope or Inter). Screen titles are bold and short. Body copy is compact and highly readable. Scientific names are italic. Section headings sit above rounded cards with clear spacing.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons:** Fully rounded, aqua teal fill, white label, generous tap height.
* **Cards:** White, 16px corners, soft shadow, image on top when the card is a species or photo card.
* **Navigation:** Persistent bottom bar with four items: Home, Fish & Nursery, Pond Management, Harvest & Records.
* **Inputs and charts:** Clean outlined fields and simple chart placeholders. Do not imply a live database.

## 5. Layout Principles
Canvas 390 × 844 px, portrait, Android status bar and home indicator. Single column. Enough white space between sections. Scroll the content. Keep the bottom navigation fixed.

## 6. Design System Notes for Stitch Generation
**Copy this block into every baton prompt:**

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Android mobile app, 390 × 844 portrait. Production app screen, not a website or slide.
- Theme: Light premium aquaculture dashboard. Deep ocean blue, aqua teal, fresh green, white, mist backgrounds.
- Background: Soft light blue (#E7F3F8)
- Primary Accent: Aqua teal (#128C7E)
- Secondary Accent: Fresh green (#3C8D4E)
- Text Primary: Deep ocean (#0B3D5C)
- Font: Clean modern sans-serif (Manrope or Inter)
- Layout: Single-column mobile screen, rounded white cards, subtle shadows, fixed four-item bottom navigation
- Photography: Realistic Indian freshwater ponds, carp, fingerlings, feeding, and farm equipment. No marine fish. No ornamental aquariums.
- Currency and units: Indian rupees (₹) and metric units.
- Accuracy: Mark farmer-reported numbers as farmer-reported. Do not invent stocking density, survival, harvest frequency, or returns. Rice bran is not rice husk. Never show a 50% daily feeding rate.
