Fish Feed & Nutrition

Supplementary Feed
Rice bran and groundnut oil cake
Traditional Supplementary Diet
1:1 Ratio

Standard formulation by weight for freshwater Indian Major Carps (Catla, Rohu, Mrigal):

Rice bran
1 part
Not rice husk
Groundnut oil cake
1 part
By weight
warning Crucial Feed Quality Advisory

Note: Rice bran and rice husk are different materials. Do not substitute rice husk for rice bran. Feed should be fresh and free from fungal contamination. Natural pond food should complement supplementary feeding.

Feeding trays

Optimal Stratum
Useful water depth 1.5 – 2.5 m

A useful water depth for carp grow-out is about 1.5–2.5 m. Choose the depth for the site and the pond design. Measure the pond in use.
