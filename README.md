# AquaFarm Pro

A Flutter guide to Indian freshwater composite carp culture of Catla (*Catla catla*), Rohu (*Labeo rohita*), and Mrigal (*Cirrhinus mrigala*).

**Smart Knowledge. Healthy Fish. Better Harvests.**

The app is a reading guide. It does not measure a pond, and the figures on screen are general reference points, not a report from one farm.

## Screens

| Tab | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Nursery and grow-out ponds, the three carps, and the four subjects |
| Fish & Nursery | Feeding zones, rearing stages, and nursery care |
| Pond Mgmt | Rice bran and groundnut oil cake, a sample feed sum, and water guidance |
| Harvest & Records | Health signs and the steps for taking fish to market |

## Run

This package targets Windows and web.

```bash
flutter pub get
flutter run -d chrome
```

`flutter test` runs the widget test.

## Guidance in the app

Stocking size around 8–12 cm, grow-out depth around 1.5–2.5 m, dissolved oxygen about 5 mg/L or higher, and pH about 6.5–8.5 are guidelines. The feed example is 100 kg of fish at 2 percent, which is 2 kg for the day. That rate is an illustration. Rice bran is not rice husk. Doses, stocking numbers, and treatments belong with a local fisheries adviser.
